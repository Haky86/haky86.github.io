# About Haky86

Haky86 is dedicated to providing AOSP builds of AOSP Custom ROMs like LineageOS, Android tutorials, and other helpful resources.

# 🛠️ What Does a LineageOS Maintainer Do?

* Maintaining a device-specific code such as (device trees, kernel, and proprietary blobs).
* Providing updates and bug fixes.

# 📲️ Supported Devices:

* Samsung Galaxy A23 5G (Active).
* Samsung Galaxy A70 (Discontinued).
* Samsung Galaxy A71 (Active).
